Little Day Tower privacy policy

Updated 18 October 2026
Publisher: Perpetuum Mobile

Little Day Tower is an offline game for young children. The game does not collect or send personal information to us. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, chat, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to the internet, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Saved on your device

The game saves room progress, completed activities, your sticker picture, sound and motion settings, and the last room you visited. It records the time of room visits locally. The tower clock uses your device's time. None of this information is sent to Perpetuum Mobile.

Keeping and deleting progress

Progress remains in the app's private storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. There is no account or server copy to delete. Clearing storage removes saved progress and settings. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

Children

We do not ask children to provide names, email addresses, photos, or other personal information. A parent or guardian should handle any support contact.

Google Play

Google operates the store and handles downloads under its own privacy policy. Store services are separate from the game's offline play.

Contact

Parents and guardians can contact Perpetuum Mobile at contact@sergiumuresan.ro. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request. Please do not include a child's personal information.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date.
